01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Research with purpose.
Teaching with clarity.

I am a PhD research scholar in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Kharagpur, supervised by Dr. Nirmalya Ghosh and jointly supervised by Dr. Shyamal Kumar Das Mandal.

My work brings together statistical signal processing and deep learning for multilingual speech enhancement, with a particular interest in Indian languages and low-resource settings. Alongside research, I develop learning resources and mentor students through Ganith Online.

02 / RESEARCH & PROJECTS

Helping speech come through.

From classical algorithms to neural architectures, my work explores speech quality, multilingual robustness, and reproducible evaluation.

01

Neural speech enhancement

MRAN-UNet explores physics-informed harmonic frequency attention. My doctoral work also includes GAN, Transformer, and CycleGAN architectures: PAGAN, HiTA-Former, and MHFA-CycleNet.
